National priorities

Renewal and growth for a better South Africa

The Medium-Term Development Plan centres government on three strategic priorities for the Seventh Administration.

01

Inclusive growth & job creation

Accelerate economic reform, infrastructure investment, industrial growth and pathways to work.
02

Reduce poverty & the cost of living

Strengthen the social wage, support households and improve access to essential services.
03

A capable, ethical & developmental state

Build institutions that are professional, accountable, effective and trusted by citizens.

The Presidency

Leadership at the centre of government

The Presidency supports the President and Deputy President in executive leadership, Cabinet coordination, national planning and oversight. The Seventh Administration began with the inauguration of President Cyril Ramaphosa on 19 June 2024.
