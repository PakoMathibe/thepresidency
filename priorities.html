Medium-Term Development Plan

Three priorities. One national programme.

The Seventh Administration is organised around a focused set of strategic priorities to improve the lives of South Africans and strengthen the state.

01

Drive inclusive growth & job creation

Grow the economy, expand investment, improve infrastructure, support industry and broaden participation in economic opportunity. This includes accelerating structural reforms through Operation Vulindlela and mobilising investment.

Key programmes: Operation Vulindlela, PICC, PES
02

Reduce poverty & tackle the high cost of living

Protect vulnerable households, strengthen the social wage and improve affordable access to essential goods and services. This includes expanding the social protection system and improving service delivery.

Key programmes: Social wage, DDM, Basic services
03

Build a capable, ethical & developmental state

Professionalise public administration, fight corruption and ensure institutions deliver with integrity and competence. This includes strengthening the criminal justice system and rebuilding state capacity.

Key programmes: State reform, Anti-corruption, SIU
How delivery happens

From policy to measurable outcomes

The Presidency coordinates government action through a structured process of planning, implementation, monitoring and reporting.

Coordinate

Align Cabinet, departments, provinces, municipalities and social partners around shared outcomes and priorities.

Implement

Use flagship programmes and reform initiatives to turn national priorities into concrete action on the ground.

Measure

Track implementation through public plans, performance measures, oversight mechanisms and independent evaluation.

Report

Communicate progress openly through speeches, statements, publications, Parliamentary engagements and the media.
