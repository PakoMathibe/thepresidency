Delivery programmes

Programmes that move South Africa forward

Cross-government initiatives coordinated or supported by The Presidency to accelerate reform, investment and public-service delivery.

Operation Vulindlela

Joint reform programme focused on overcoming binding constraints to economic growth. Coordinated by The Presidency and National Treasury.

Learn more

District Development Model

Integrated planning and delivery across the three spheres of government, centred on districts as the primary locus of service delivery.

Infrastructure coordination

Supporting strategic infrastructure investment and implementation across the economy through the Presidential Infrastructure Coordinating Commission.

Presidential Employment Stimulus

Supporting public employment, livelihoods and pathways into work for unemployed South Africans, particularly young people.

Government–Business Partnership

Mobilising business capability alongside government to address priority constraints and accelerate economic recovery.

Climate coordination

Supporting a just transition and coherent national responses to climate change, including adaptation, mitigation and green growth.

State-owned enterprise reform

Strengthening the governance, performance and strategic role of public enterprises, with a focus on energy, logistics and transport.

Youth development

Coordinating opportunities for learning, employment, entrepreneurship and participation across government, business and civil society.
