The Seventh Administration

Leadership for a democratic South Africa

The inauguration of President Cyril Ramaphosa on 19 June 2024 marked the beginning of the Seventh Administration. The Presidency supports the President and Deputy President in leading a government that works for all South Africans.

🇿🇦 President of South Africa
President

President Cyril Ramaphosa

Head of State and Government of the Republic of South Africa. The Presidency supports the President in executive leadership, coordination of government, international representation and constitutional responsibilities.

Institution

The strategic centre of government

The Presidency coordinates the work of government across four key functional areas.

Executive support

Support to the President and Deputy President in carrying out constitutional and executive responsibilities.

Cabinet coordination

Administrative and strategic support to Cabinet decision-making and implementation.

Planning & oversight

Coordination of priorities, implementation tracking and government-wide performance monitoring.

Public communication

Official information, speeches, statements, public engagements and media services.

History

Seventh Administration milestones

19 Jun 2024
Inauguration

President Cyril Ramaphosa inaugurated for the Seventh Administration at the Union Buildings.

Jul 2024
Cabinet announcement

New Cabinet sworn in, marking the start of the Government of National Unity.

Aug 2024
Opening of Parliament

President addresses the nation on the Seventh Administration's priorities and plans.

Mar 2025
Medium-Term Development Plan

Launch of the MTDP 2025–2030, setting out the three strategic priorities.

2026
Delivering on priorities

Implementation continues across economic reform, poverty reduction and state capacity building.
